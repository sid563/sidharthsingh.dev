## Why

I wanted to own every step from `git init` to a public URL, including the parts a platform usually hides: security groups, SSH, running a container on a server, and what breaks when the instance restarts.

## What it does

- Ingests documents, chunks them, embeds them with a Hugging Face model and stores vectors in MongoDB Atlas.
- Answers questions over that corpus with a Llama model served by Groq.
- Runs as a single Docker container on an EC2 instance.

## Loose ends I know about

No Elastic IP yet, no domain or TLS, and the deploy is a manual pull. Fixing those is part of the [cloud and DevOps roadmap](/roadmap).
