The button in the corner now opens a real chat. It runs on my [personal RAG service](/projects/personal-rag): a knowledge base of about a hundred chunks written from this site's pages, embedded with bge-small on Cloudflare Workers AI, retrieved from MongoDB Atlas Vector Search with a similarity threshold, and answered by gpt-oss-120b on Groq with Llama on Cloudflare as the fallback. Below the threshold it says it does not know and points at my email instead of guessing.

Still to do: tune the threshold against real questions, fill in the FAQ chunks the site cannot answer on its own, and keep the knowledge base in step with the pages.
