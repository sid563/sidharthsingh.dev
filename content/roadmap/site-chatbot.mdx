Shipped on 29 October 2026. The button in the corner opens a chat backed by my [personal RAG service](/projects/personal-rag): a hundred hand-written chunks embedded with bge-small on Cloudflare Workers AI, retrieved from MongoDB Atlas Vector Search behind a similarity threshold, answered by gpt-oss-120b on Groq with Llama on Cloudflare as the fallback, and a Turnstile browser check plus shared rate limits in front. Below the threshold it says it does not know and points at my email instead of guessing.

Next: keep the knowledge base in step with the pages as they change, and fill in the questions the site cannot answer on its own.
